# StitchLink

StitchLink is a web-based marketplace that connects clothing shops with solo and home-based dressmakers.

The platform helps shops find suitable dressmakers for clothing orders while allowing dressmakers to showcase their skills, availability, capacity, and previous work.

## Project Goals

- Connect clothing shops with independent dressmakers
- Simplify clothing order requests and quotations
- Help shops find suitable dressmakers
- Support dressmakers in marketing their services
- Use AI to assist with matching, quotations, capacity, demand, and production risk
- Provide analytics to support better business decisions
