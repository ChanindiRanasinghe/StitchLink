\# StitchLink Users and Stakeholders



\## 1. Clothing Shop



Clothing shops are businesses that need dressmaking capacity for their clothing orders.



\### Main needs

\- Create clothing order requests

\- Specify order requirements

\- Discover suitable dressmakers

\- Compare quotations

\- Select dressmakers

\- Track order progress

\- Communicate with dressmakers

\- Review completed work



\---



\## 2. Solo / Home-Based Dressmaker



Solo and home-based dressmakers are independent individuals who accept clothing production work.



\### Main needs

\- Create a professional profile

\- Showcase skills and portfolio

\- Specify specialization and experience

\- Set availability and production capacity

\- Discover suitable shop orders

\- Receive and respond to order opportunities

\- Submit quotations

\- Manage accepted orders

\- Communicate with shops

\- Build reputation through reviews



\---



\## 3. System Administrator



The administrator manages and monitors the StitchLink platform.



\### Main responsibilities

\- Manage user accounts

\- Verify dressmaker and shop information

\- Manage user roles and permissions

\- Monitor marketplace activities

\- Manage clothing categories

\- Monitor orders and quotations

\- Handle reported issues or disputes

\- Suspend or deactivate accounts when necessary

\- View system statistics and analytics



\---



\## Stakeholder Summary



| Stakeholder | Main Interest |

|---|---|

| Clothing Shop | Find suitable dressmakers and manage clothing production orders |

| Dressmaker | Find shop opportunities and manage production work |

| System Administrator | Maintain platform security, verification, and marketplace operations |



\## Primary Users



The two primary users of StitchLink are:



1\. Clothing shops

2\. Solo / home-based dressmakers



The system administrator is responsible for platform administration and management.
