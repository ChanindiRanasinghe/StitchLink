\# StitchLink Problem Statement



\## Background



Many solo and home-based dressmakers operate independently and rely on personal contacts, social media, word-of-mouth recommendations, or informal communication to find clothing orders. At the same time, clothing shops may need additional dressmaking capacity when they receive orders that cannot be completed by their existing staff.



The lack of a dedicated platform connecting these two groups can make it difficult for shops to discover suitable dressmakers and for independent dressmakers to access new business opportunities.



\## Problem



Clothing shops may struggle to identify suitable independent dressmakers based on skills, experience, location, availability, production capacity, pricing, and previous work.



Independent and home-based dressmakers may also have difficulty reaching potential clothing shops, promoting their capabilities, finding suitable orders, and managing business opportunities in a structured way.



Existing communication methods such as phone calls, messaging applications, social media, and informal referrals do not provide a centralized marketplace for discovering, comparing, matching, and managing these opportunities.



\## Proposed Solution



StitchLink is a web-based marketplace designed to connect clothing shops with solo and home-based dressmakers.



The platform will allow dressmakers to create professional profiles, showcase their skills and previous work, provide availability and capacity information, and discover suitable order opportunities.



Clothing shops will be able to create order requests, discover suitable dressmakers, compare quotations, and manage orders through the platform.



StitchLink will also incorporate intelligent features to assist with dressmaker matching, quotation estimation, capacity prediction, demand prediction, and production risk identification.



\## Main Goal



The main goal of StitchLink is to provide a centralized digital platform that improves the connection between clothing shops and independent dressmakers while supporting more efficient order discovery, matching, quotation, and production management.
