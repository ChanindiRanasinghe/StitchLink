\# StitchLink Project Objectives



\## Main Objective



To develop a web-based marketplace that connects clothing shops with solo and home-based dressmakers and supports the discovery, matching, quotation, and management of clothing production opportunities.



\## Specific Objectives



1\. To provide dressmakers with professional profiles where they can showcase their skills, experience, specialization, portfolio, availability, and production capacity.



2\. To allow clothing shops to create and manage clothing order requests with relevant requirements such as clothing category, quantity, deadline, budget, and specifications.



3\. To provide a structured marketplace where clothing shops can discover and compare suitable dressmakers.



4\. To develop an intelligent matching mechanism that recommends suitable dressmakers based on factors such as skills, experience, location, availability, capacity, pricing, and performance.



5\. To provide an AI-assisted quotation feature that can help estimate suitable production costs and completion times.



6\. To provide capacity prediction capabilities that help identify whether a dressmaker has sufficient capacity to accept additional work.



7\. To provide demand prediction capabilities that can identify patterns in clothing order demand.



8\. To identify potential production risks such as approaching deadlines, workload issues, and delayed progress.



9\. To support the quotation and order lifecycle from order requests through quotation, acceptance, production, and completion.



10\. To provide communication and notification features for important order and production updates.



11\. To provide reviews and ratings that contribute to dressmaker reputation and future recommendations.



12\. To provide an administrative interface for managing users, verification, marketplace activities, and system information.



13\. To provide analytics that can help understand marketplace activity, order trends, dressmaker performance, and demand patterns.



14\. To develop the system using a scalable architecture that separates the frontend, backend, database, and intelligent services.
