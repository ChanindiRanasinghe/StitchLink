\# StitchLink Functional Requirements



\## 1. User Registration and Authentication



\- The system shall allow users to register as a Clothing Shop or Dressmaker.

\- The system shall allow registered users to log in and log out.

\- The system shall provide password recovery functionality.

\- The system shall authenticate users securely.

\- The system shall restrict features based on user roles.



\## 2. Dressmaker Profile Management



\- The system shall allow dressmakers to create and update their profiles.

\- The system shall allow dressmakers to provide their skills and specialization.

\- The system shall allow dressmakers to provide experience information.

\- The system shall allow dressmakers to specify location.

\- The system shall allow dressmakers to specify pricing information.

\- The system shall allow dressmakers to specify production capacity.

\- The system shall allow dressmakers to specify availability.

\- The system shall allow dressmakers to upload portfolio items.



\## 3. Clothing Shop Profile Management



\- The system shall allow clothing shops to create and update business profiles.

\- The system shall allow shops to provide business information and location.

\- The system shall allow shops to manage their order preferences.



\## 4. Marketplace and Order Requests



\- The system shall allow shops to create clothing order requests.

\- Order requests shall contain relevant requirements such as category, quantity, deadline, budget, specifications, and reference images.

\- The system shall allow shops to update or cancel eligible order requests.

\- The system shall allow dressmakers to browse available opportunities.

\- The system shall allow dressmakers to search and filter suitable orders.

\- The system shall allow dressmakers to submit applications for suitable orders.



\## 5. Dressmaker Matching



\- The system shall recommend suitable dressmakers for shop order requests.

\- Matching shall consider factors such as skills, experience, location, availability, capacity, pricing, and performance.

\- The system shall provide a matching score or ranking for recommended dressmakers.



\## 6. Quotations



\- The system shall allow dressmakers to submit quotations for order requests.

\- Quotations shall include estimated material costs, labour costs, additional costs, total price, estimated completion time, and notes.

\- Shops shall be able to review submitted quotations.

\- Shops shall be able to accept or reject quotations.



\## 7. AI-Assisted Quotation



\- The system shall provide AI-assisted cost and time estimation.

\- The system shall use relevant order information when generating estimates.

\- The system shall present AI-generated estimates as recommendations rather than guaranteed prices.



\## 8. Order Management



\- The system shall maintain an order lifecycle.

\- Orders shall support statuses such as Requested, Quoted, Accepted, In Production, Completed, and Cancelled.

\- Shops and dressmakers shall be able to view relevant order information.

\- The system shall provide order progress information.



\## 9. Capacity Prediction



\- The system shall analyse dressmaker workload and availability.

\- The system shall estimate whether a dressmaker has sufficient capacity for additional work.

\- The system shall provide workload or capacity warnings where appropriate.



\## 10. Demand Prediction



\- The system shall analyse historical order information.

\- The system shall identify demand patterns by relevant categories, locations, or time periods.

\- The system shall provide demand predictions to support planning.



\## 11. Production Risk Detection



\- The system shall monitor relevant order information such as deadlines, progress, workload, and delays.

\- The system shall identify potential production risks.

\- The system shall provide risk indicators or warnings.



\## 12. Messaging and Notifications



\- The system shall allow shops and dressmakers to communicate regarding orders.

\- The system shall provide notifications for important events such as new orders, quotations, accepted quotations, updates, deadlines, and risk warnings.



\## 13. Reviews and Ratings



\- The system shall allow eligible shops and dressmakers to review completed transactions.

\- The system shall support ratings and written reviews.

\- The system shall calculate reputation information from valid reviews.

\- The system shall prevent duplicate reviews for the same transaction.



\## 14. Administration



\- The system shall provide an administrative dashboard.

\- Administrators shall be able to manage users.

\- Administrators shall be able to verify relevant user information.

\- Administrators shall be able to manage clothing categories.

\- Administrators shall be able to monitor orders and marketplace activities.

\- Administrators shall be able to manage reported issues or disputes.

\- Administrators shall be able to suspend or deactivate accounts where appropriate.



\## 15. Analytics



\- The system shall collect relevant marketplace activity data.

\- The system shall provide statistics and reports.

\- Analytics shall include relevant information such as orders, dressmaker activity, demand trends, and marketplace performance.
